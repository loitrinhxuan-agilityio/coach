## The quality gates

As proposed, and **not approved**:

1. Required automated tests pass.
2. The UI conforms to the approved design.
3. An independent reviewer approves the change.

They stay proposals until they are reviewed and approved. Item 6 no longer comes first — CI exists —
but two things still do, and they compound:

1. **The checks are advisory until they are required on `main`.** That is a branch-protection setting,
   not a file, so it is the Owner's to set.
2. **Requiring them is not enough on its own**, because `independent-review.yml` exits 0 even when it
   posts findings. A required check that always passes is a gate in name only. Item 6 carries the
   proposed fix.

Approving the gates before either is done records an intention, not a control.
