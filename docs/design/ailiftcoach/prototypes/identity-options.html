Three identity directions

The same fragment — stat tiles, a set table, provenance chips, a coaching rule — drawn three ways, each in light and dark. Structure, spacing and components are identical throughout; only palette and type change. Pick one and I will carry it through the system and both prototypes.

A Instrument Current

Cool grey with one signal blue. The quietest of the three: the figures are the loudest thing on the page, and colour appears only where it means something. Safest, and the most likely to look like other dashboards.

C Graphite and copper

Warm graphite with a desaturated copper. The most physical of the three — it reads as equipment rather than software, which suits a product about loaded bars. It is the closest to ShredPace's warm family, though copper is darker and far less saturated than its orange, and the type and geometry are nothing alike.

What does not change, whichever you pick

Chroma means something. One accent for interaction, a state ramp for status, a series ramp for data. Only the hues move.

Mono for every measurement. The split between language and measurement is structural, so each direction keeps a sans and a mono — only the pairing changes.

Square geometry and hairlines. 10px maximum corner, 3px chips, rules rather than boxes.

The IWF plate colours stay fixed in every direction. A 20 kg plate is blue whatever the brand does.
