Today

The comeback question, asked once on the first session day back, together with the check-in. Answer your agent, or answer here. The three kinds below are three days, not three cards.

Short break · up to 3 weeks

Back after 4 days off

Same weights as last time, or ease in?

If same: Same weights as last time, as you said. Each set follows what you lift.

Long break · over 3 weeks · ease in is offered first

Back after 5 weeks off

Ease in, or same weights as last time?

If ease in: Easing in, about 10% lighter, as you said. Each set follows what you lift.

After being ill · replaces “Fully recovered?”

Back after 10 days off ill

Ease in, or same weights as last time if you feel fully recovered?

Easing after illness is about 20% lighter, not 10%. Saying “same” also ends the lighter week.
