Privacy

Updated 24 October 2026 · Make a privacy request ›

Who’s responsible

Shredpace is operated by a named company, which decides how your data is used: the controller, under the GDPR. Write to the privacy mailbox, or use the contact form.

What’s stored

Only what the app needs to keep your training log: your email address, kept by Amazon Web Services to sign you in; your workouts, settings, plans, programs and goals, stored in the EU (Ireland); what you write in workout notes, as you wrote it; and your height, year of birth and body type if you give them.

If you agree: body readings, meals, check-ins, your health-check answers, injuries and limitations, time away when ill or injured, dietary needs, the numbers you track, aches and pains, and what you tell your agent about a session. Photos and screenshots are never stored.

API keys are stored hashed: the key is shown to you once, and only its name, its first 8 characters and when it last called are kept. Error logs are kept for one month. Contact messages are kept until a year after they are resolved. Billing records are kept for 6 years, because tax law requires it.

Signing in with Google

If you choose Continue with Google, Google gives Shredpace your name, your email address and your Google account ID, and nothing else. They are used only to create your account and sign you in, and are never sold, shared or used for advertising. Delete your account to remove them, or revoke access at your Google account’s permissions page.

Health details and your consent

Body readings, meals, check-ins, injuries and the rest of the list above are health details: special category data under the GDPR. They are kept only with your explicit consent, given once, on the dashboard or at the link your agent sends you.

Turn it off in Settings › Privacy and they are deleted at once, including the copies in the activity log. A record of each time you agreed or turned it off is kept as proof, with the words you saw, for as long as the account exists and 3 years after. When the words change, you are asked again: until you answer, your health details are paused, and after 30 days they are deleted.

Why we may use it

To run the service you signed up for (a contract); health details only with your explicit consent; messages and error logs to answer you and keep the service secure (a legitimate interest); and billing records because the law requires them.

Your agents

Your agents read and write your log through the API keys you create. A key can log and read workouts. It can’t manage keys or your account, and it can’t agree to keep health details for you. Revoke a key in Settings and it stops working at once.

What we don’t do

Your data is never sold or shared. Amazon Web Services processes it for us in the EU (Ireland), only on our instructions. There are no analytics, trackers or ads: the pages load only the app’s own scripts and fonts.

Export or delete

Export from Settings (JSON; CSV comes with Pro). Delete your account in Settings › Account and everything in it goes at once. A paid plan is cancelled with no refund; only the consent record (3 years) and billing records (6 years) stay.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, deletion, or to restrict or object to how it is used. We answer within one month. You can take your data with you, withdraw consent at any time, and complain to a data protection authority.
